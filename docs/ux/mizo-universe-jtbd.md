# Jobs-to-be-Done Analysis: Mizo Universe Portfolio

## User Persona

**Who**: Recruiters, potential collaborators, sports brands, tech investors, and fans who encounter Captain Mizo Amin's digital presence.

**Role breakdown**:
- **Talent Scout / Recruiter**: Evaluating Mizo as an athlete, influencer, or brand ambassador
- **Tech Collaborator**: Assessing Mizo's technical capabilities and project portfolio
- **Brand Partner**: Determining sponsorship or collaboration fit
- **Fan / Follower**: Learning more about Mizo's story and achievements

**Skill level**: Mixed — ranges from tech-savvy developers to non-technical sports executives

**Primary device**: Desktop (immersive 3D), mobile (quick lookups between meetings)

**Accessibility needs**: Must support keyboard navigation; high contrast is important for stadium/outdoor viewing conditions

**Tech-savviness**: Moderate — comfortable with websites but not necessarily with 3D interfaces

---

## Job Statements

### Primary Job

> **When** I encounter Captain Mizo Amin's name — through social media, a referral, or press coverage — **I want to** quickly understand who he is, what he has accomplished, and whether his brand aligns with my goals, **so I can** decide whether to reach out, collaborate, invest, or follow his journey.

### Secondary Jobs

**For recruiters / talent scouts**:
> **When** I'm evaluating a multi-disciplinary athlete-entrepreneur for a partnership or sponsorship, **I want to** see concrete achievements, personality, and proof of work in one place, **so I can** make a confident decision without scheduling multiple calls.

**For tech collaborators**:
> **When** I'm exploring potential tech partners or contributors, **I want to** understand Mizo's technical depth and projects, **so I can** determine if there is a strong basis for collaboration.

**For fans / followers**:
> **When** I want to learn more about Mizo Amin beyond social media posts, **I want to** explore his world in an engaging and memorable way, **so I can** feel a deeper connection to his story and share it with others.

---

## Functional vs. Emotional Jobs

| Dimension | Job |
|-----------|-----|
| **Functional** | Find contact details, review achievements, understand skills and projects |
| **Emotional** | Feel impressed and inspired; leave with a clear mental model of who Mizo is |
| **Social** | Be able to share or reference Mizo's portfolio confidently to colleagues |

---

## Current Solution & Pain Points

### What visitors use today
- Mizo's social media profiles (Instagram, LinkedIn, X/Twitter)
- Third-party sports databases and press articles
- Generic portfolio sites or LinkedIn profile

### Why these solutions fail

| Current Solution | Pain Point |
|-----------------|------------|
| Social media | Fragmented — achievements scattered across platforms, no narrative |
| Press articles | Outdated, lack depth on tech and business work |
| LinkedIn | Formal and static; doesn't convey energy or personality |
| Generic portfolio | Template-driven, forgettable, doesn't stand out in a crowded field |

### Consequence of failure
- Visitor can't form a complete picture → exits without reaching out
- Mizo loses high-value collaboration opportunities due to inadequate first impressions
- Brand partnerships go to competitors with more polished digital presence

## Success Criteria (JTBD Outcomes)

| Outcome | Metric |
|---------|--------|
| Visitor immediately understands Mizo's multi-domain identity | Time-to-comprehension < 60 seconds |
| Visitor feels engaged enough to explore deeper | Average session time > 2 minutes |
| Visitor finds a clear call-to-action | Contact/follow conversion rate > 5% |
| Visitor remembers the experience | Return visit rate > 20% |

## Incumbent Analysis

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness vs. Mizo Universe |
|-----------|----------|---------------------------|
| Standard portfolio sites | Familiar UX | Generic, low memorability |
| LinkedIn | Professional credibility | Dry, algorithm-dependent |
| Social media | Reach and frequency | No depth, no narrative control |
| Sports agency pages | Niche credibility | No personality, not self-owned |

**Mizo Universe differentiator**: An immersive, self-owned 3D experience that fuses the athlete's sports, tech, and business identities into a single, memorable narrative — hiring the product for "making me feel like I understand this person completely."
